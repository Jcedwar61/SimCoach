4. Online services
------------------

Microsoft's online speech service (the Microsoft voices), Google Translate's speech (the Google voices) and,
with your own key, OpenAI, Google Gemini and DeepSeek. Their terms of service apply to their use. Nothing is
sent to them but the text being spoken or the race summary the AI commentators write from.
